# Bound for the Peak

This is an endless physics-based expedition up a mountain built in Godot 4.7.2 for Hack Club Terra Week 5, following the them "Peak".
You control twin climbers tethered to each other on an infinite mountain that generates as you play. 
You can only control one climber at a time, so strategically anchor and toss them up the mountain and ride air streams 
while dodging falling icicles, jumping on fragile platforms, and more! You can only mess up three times before you fall for good.
Try and climb as high as possible and plant your flag! You can retry to beat your personal best!

Play in your browser!: https://praneet-mudgala.github.io/Bound-for-the-Peak/

## Controls and Mechanics

A / D OR Left and Right Arrows: Walking and swinging on rope to the left and right
W OR Up Arrow: Jumping and swinging on rope upwards
Space: Anchor the active climber (if possible) and swap climbers
E: Swap climbers
Q: Unanchor climbers
F: Toss up inactive climber (if both are unanchored and close to each other) and swap climbers
Esc: Pause menu

3-Heart Lifesaver: A lifesaver will rescue you if you fall or take damage up to three times.

## Mountain Terrain

Snow and rocks: Stable surface that you can anchor and walk on
Slippery blue ice: Stable surface with less friction that you can walk on but cannot anchor on
Fragile ice: Unstable surface, you can walk anchor and walk on it but it will break under your weight
Falling icicles: Icicles that will fall on you when you walk underneath them
Updraft wind: Strong wind that will blow an unanchored climber up

## Running locally

1. Clone or download this repository
2. Open Godot Engine 4.7.2 Stable and import the project.godot file
3. Explore the code or press f5 to launch the game locally
